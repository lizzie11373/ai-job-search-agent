# Senior AI Product Engineer — Northstar Labs

## About the Role
You will build AI-powered product experiences used by operations teams to research, summarize, and act on complex information.

## Responsibilities
- Build and improve production applications powered by large language models.
- Design retrieval-augmented generation workflows for grounded question answering.
- Partner with product and design to translate user problems into AI product requirements.
- Develop offline evaluations for answer quality, grounding, and task completion.
- Instrument AI features with latency, failure-rate, and usage metrics.

## Minimum Qualifications
- 4+ years of professional software development experience.
- Strong Python programming skills.
- Experience building applications with large language models or other generative AI systems.
- Experience designing or consuming production APIs.
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related technical field, or equivalent practical experience.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with vector databases.
- Experience with prompt evaluation or LLM evaluation frameworks.
- Experience building internal tools for knowledge workers.
