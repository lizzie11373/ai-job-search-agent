# AI Product Manager — Harbor AI

## The Opportunity
You will own a product area focused on AI-assisted decision support for enterprise users.

## What You'll Do
- Define product strategy and roadmaps for generative-AI features.
- Translate customer research into prioritized product requirements.
- Partner with engineering to define evaluation criteria before launch.
- Review qualitative and quantitative product signals after release and decide what to improve.

## Qualifications
- You must have 4+ years of product management experience.
- Demonstrated experience shipping at least one software product from discovery through launch.
- Strong ability to communicate tradeoffs with engineering, design, and business stakeholders.
- You should be comfortable using SQL or another analytics tool to investigate product behavior.

## Additional Experience
- Experience with LLM-powered products is preferred.
- Experience with enterprise SaaS would be helpful.
- Prior work with evaluation datasets or human review workflows is a plus.
- An MBA is not required.
