# Technical Product Manager, AI Platform — Meridian Cloud

## Role Summary
This role requires strong cross-functional communication, comfort working with data, and experience defining AI product requirements.

## Responsibilities
- Define requirements for AI platform capabilities with engineering and design.
- Use product analytics and customer feedback to prioritize roadmap decisions.
- Communicate product tradeoffs with engineering, design, sales, and customer-success teams.
- Define success metrics and evaluation plans for new AI capabilities.

## Minimum Qualifications
- 5+ years of product management experience.
- Experience defining product requirements for technical products.
- Strong cross-functional communication skills.
- Experience using SQL or product analytics tools.
- Experience defining metrics for product launches.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with generative AI products is preferred.
- Experience with model evaluation or experimentation is preferred.
- Strong cross-functional communication is a plus.
- Experience using analytics to prioritize product decisions is preferred.
