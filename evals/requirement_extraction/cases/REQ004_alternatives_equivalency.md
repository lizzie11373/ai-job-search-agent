# Applied AI Engineer — Summit Compute

## Responsibilities
- Build and operate services that use large language models for document analysis.
- Create evaluation datasets and regression tests for AI behavior.
- Collaborate with platform engineers to deploy and observe model-backed services.

## Required Qualifications
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, or a related field, OR equivalent practical experience.
- 3+ years of professional programming experience in Python or Go.
- Experience deploying services on at least one major cloud platform: AWS, GCP, or Azure.
- Experience with SQL and relational databases.
- Experience using Git in a collaborative development environment.

## Experience Equivalency
A relevant master's degree may substitute for up to one year of the professional programming experience requirement.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Experience with Kubernetes is preferred.
- Familiarity with LLM evaluation, retrieval systems, or vector databases is a plus.
