# Clinical AI Implementation Specialist — Bridge Health Systems

## Role Responsibilities
- Lead implementation of AI-assisted clinical workflow tools at hospital sites.
- Train clinical users and gather feedback on workflow usability.
- Partner with engineering and clinical operations to investigate safety or workflow issues.
- Document implementation risks and communicate escalation needs.

## Required Qualifications
- Active, unrestricted Registered Nurse (RN) license in at least one U.S. state.
- 3+ years of direct clinical experience in an acute-care hospital environment.
- Experience working with electronic health record workflows.
- Ability to travel up to 30% to customer hospital sites.
- Bachelor's degree in Nursing or a related clinical field.

## Preferred Qualifications
- Epic certification is preferred.
- PMP certification is a plus.
- Experience implementing clinical software is preferred.
- Familiarity with AI-assisted clinical decision-support tools is preferred.
