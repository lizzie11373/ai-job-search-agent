# Forward Deployed AI Engineer — Atlas Decision Systems

## About the Role
Customers bring us ambiguous operational problems rather than fully specified software requirements. You will work directly with customer teams to understand the problem, identify what data and systems are available, and turn that context into working AI-enabled solutions. A typical engagement may involve prototyping quickly, validating the approach with real users, then hardening the workflow with the platform engineering team.

Success in this role depends on being able to move between customer conversations and hands-on technical work. You will be expected to explain technical tradeoffs clearly, write production-quality code when needed, and determine when an LLM-based approach is appropriate versus when a simpler deterministic solution is better.

## Background We Are Looking For
We require at least 3 years of professional software engineering or applied machine-learning experience. You must be able to program effectively in Python and work with SQL-based data systems. Experience integrating third-party or internal APIs is required.

Because this role is customer-facing, you must be comfortable leading technical discovery sessions and presenting technical recommendations to mixed technical and non-technical audiences. The role requires travel to customer sites up to 25%.

Experience deploying LLM applications is preferred. Experience with retrieval-augmented generation, evaluation frameworks, or tool-calling agents would be a plus. Prior consulting experience is helpful but not required.

## Working Style
We value people who are curious, pragmatic, and willing to challenge assumptions. We do not expect candidates to know every model framework or vendor-specific SDK.
