# Product Engineer, AI Workflow Tools — Juniper Works

## About Juniper Works
We believe software should make complicated work feel simple. Our team values curiosity, kindness, thoughtful debate, and learning in public.

You will join a small cross-functional group building tools for operations teams. The team works closely together and frequently shares prototypes with internal users.

## Your Work
- Build AI-assisted workflow features from prototype through production.
- Interview internal users and incorporate feedback into product iterations.
- Create lightweight quality checks for AI-generated outputs.
- Investigate production issues across application code and model behavior.

- 3+ years of software product development experience.
- Proficiency in Python.
- Experience working directly with users or product partners.
- No prior healthcare experience is required.
- You do not need prior experience with a specific LLM framework.
